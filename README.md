# MochiMochi Player

A modern, all-in-one visual novel and RPG player for Android. Play your favorite PC story-rich games directly on your mobile device with smooth performance, built-in cheat tools, and full gamepad support.

---

## 🎮 Supported Engines

- **Ren'Py**: Full support for Ren'Py games (both legacy and modern versions), including visual novels with **Live2D** animated models.
- **RPG Maker**: Play games made in **RPG Maker MV** and **RPG Maker MZ**.
- **TyranoScript**: Play interactive visual novels and sound novels.

---

## ⚡ Built-in Cheats & Save Tools

- **Live Variable Editor**: Browse and edit in-game variables (gold, stats, items, points) in real time while playing.
- **Variable Freezing**: Lock values in place so your money, health, or stamina never decrease.
- **Save Editor**: Tweak and modify your game saves directly within the app without needing external tools.

---

## 🕹️ Controls & Gamepad Support

- **Physical Gamepad Support**: Plug-and-play support for Bluetooth and USB gamepads (Xbox, PlayStation, and generic controllers) with vibration and rumble feedback.
- **Customizable Virtual Controls**: On-screen buttons and d-pad tailored for touch devices, with customizable layouts and sizing.
- **Touch-Friendly Navigation**: Quick gestures for dialogue advancement, text skipping, auto-play, and menu navigation.

---

## ✨ Features & Customization

- **Game Library**: Organize all your games in a clean, visual bookshelf.
- **Custom Artwork**: Set your own custom cover art and banner images for each game.
- **Fast & Smooth**: Instant game resume, smooth animations, and optimized loading times.
