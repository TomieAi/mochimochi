# MochiMochi Player

A modern, all-in-one visual novel and RPG player for Android. Play your favorite PC story-rich games directly on your mobile device with smooth performance and built-in cheat tools.

---

## 🎮 Supported Engines

- **Ren'Py**: Full support for Ren'Py visual novels (both legacy and modern versions), including games with **Live2D** animated models.
- **RPG Maker**: Play games made in **RPG Maker MV** and **RPG Maker MZ**.
- **TyranoScript**: Play interactive visual novels and sound novels.

---

## ⚡ Built-in Cheats & Save Tools

- **Live Variable Editor**: Browse and edit in-game values (gold, stats, items, points) in real time while playing.
- **Variable Freezing**: Lock values in place so your money, health, or stamina never decrease.
- **Save Editor**: Tweak and modify your game saves directly within the app without needing external tools.

---

## 🕹️ Controls & Navigation

- **Customizable Virtual Controls**: On-screen buttons and d-pad tailored for touch devices, with customizable layouts and positioning.
- **Touch-Friendly Gestures**: Quick tap and swipe gestures for dialogue advancement, text skipping, auto-play, and menu navigation.

---

## ✨ Features & Customization

- **Game Library**: Organize all your games in a clean, visual bookshelf.
- **Custom Artwork**: Set your own custom cover art and banner images for each game.
- **Fast & Smooth**: Instant game resume, smooth animations, and optimized loading times.
